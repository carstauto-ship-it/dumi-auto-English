About DUMI AUTO

DUMI AUTO is a China-based supplier focused on automotive protection films. Our range covers paint protection film (PPF), window tint and solar film, color change wrap film, and ceramic coating and sealants.

We work with trade customers rather than retail car owners: distributors, installers, wrap shops, detailers, fleet buyers and online resellers sourcing films for their own businesses and end customers.

Product specifications, material data, samples and volume pricing are provided on request so that each buyer can match the film to its market and application.
